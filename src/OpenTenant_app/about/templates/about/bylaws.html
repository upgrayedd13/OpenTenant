LPM Union Bylaws

Mission Statement ¶

The LPM Residents' Union is a tenants' association organized in accordance with Minnesota Statutes §504B.001 and §504B.212. Its purpose is to unite tenants in collective action, promote community vibrancy, and advocate for transparent and accountable property management. The union is independently governed by participating LPM residents, for the benefit of all tenants, and operates in accordance with the terms outlined below.

Meetings ¶

Annual Meeting ¶

  1. An annual meeting for all tenants of the building will be organized once per calendar year
  2. The purpose of the meeting is to:
    1. Review the activities and financial standing of the union
    2. Hold elections
    3. Vote on proposed bylaw amendments or resolution
    4. Provide space for community discussion and tenant input
  3. Notice of the Annual Meeting must be provided to all residents at least 30 days in advance, and absentee ballots and/or remote participation options must be made available

Special Meetings ¶

  1. Special meetings of the general membership may be called by:
    1. A majority vote of the Tenants' Board
    2. A written petition signed by at least 5% of [estimated] tenants
  2. Notice must be provided at least 5 days in advance, along with the purpose and agenda of the meeting.

Notice of Meetings ¶

Method of Notice ¶

  1. Notice of Annual and Special meetings shall be provided using:
    1. The most convenient contact method provided by each member, as recorded by the Secretary (e.g. email, phone, or text), and
    2. At least one publicly accessible method, such as flyers posted in common areas, building bulletin boards, or announcements via social media.
  2. The Secretary, or another designated officer, is responsible for ensuring timely and accessible notice is delivered to all members.
  3. It is the responsibility of each member to ensure that their contact information is provided to the Secretary and kept up to date for the purposes of receiving Union communications.

Timing of Notice ¶

  1. Notice for Annual Meetings must be given at least 30 days in advance.
  2. Notice for Special Meetings must be given at least 5 days in advance.
  3. Notices must include the date, time, location (or virtual platform link), and a proposed agenda.

Tenants' Board Meetings ¶

  1. The Tenants' Board shall meet at least once every two months to manage ongoing business and organize union efforts.
  2. Meetings may be held in person or virtually.
  3. A simple majority of current Tenants' Board members shall constitute a quorum.
  4. All tenants are invited to observe board meetings.
  5. Meeting minutes shall be made available within two weeks.

Quorum for Annual and Special Meetings ¶

  1. A quorum for any meeting requiring the vote of the general membership shall consist of at least 5% of the [estimated] tenants in the building. Absentee ballots and remote participation shall count towards a quorum.

Rules of Order ¶

Meeting Procedure ¶

  1. Meetings shall follow a modified form of Robert's Rules of Order, tailored to the size and needs of the Union.
  2. The Chair is responsible for keeping the meeting on track and recognizing speakers.

Discussion ¶

  1. Members must be recognized by the Chair before speaking.
  2. Speakers shall address the group respectfully and stay on topic.
  3. Members may raise a "Point of Order" if they believe the rules are not being followed. The Chair shall rule on the point, with the option for the group to vote if disputed.

Motions and Voting ¶

  1. Any member may make a motion. Motions must be seconded to proceed to discussion and vote.
  2. Votes shall be decided by simple majority, unless otherwise specified in the bylaws.
  3. Voting may occur by voice, show of hands, or ballot, depending on the nature of the decision and size of the meeting.

Agenda and Timekeeping ¶

  1. The Chair will circulate a proposed agenda prior to the meeting.
  2. Members may propose additions or changes to the agenda at the start of the meeting.
  3. Time limits may be set for discussion of specific items if necessary to stay on schedule.

Flexibility ¶

  1. While the Union values orderly procedure, these rules may be relaxed by consensus for informal or small meetings, provided all members are treated fairly and given a chance to participate.

Membership ¶

Eligibility ¶

  1. Any current tenant or lawful occupant of LPM Apartments is eligible for membership in the LPM Residents' Union
  2. Eligibility is open to lease holders, subletters, roommates, and other long-term residents, regardless of leaseholder status.

Becoming a Member ¶

  1. To become a member, eligible tenants must complete a membership form, either digitally or in writing. Membership forms will be made available at meetings, via email, and through other accessible channels.
  2. Members are encouraged to provide a contact method (such as email or phone number) to receive meeting notices, updates, and voting materials.

Dues ¶

  1. The Union may, by vote of the Tenants' Board or general membership, establish a nominal annual due, not to exceed $5, to support outreach, printing, and organizing needs.
  2. No tenant shall be denied membership for inability to pay. A dues waiver will be granted upon request without explanation.

Rights and Privileges ¶

  1. Members in good standing have the right to
    1. Vote in union elections and on proposed resolutions
    2. Run for office or serve on committees
    3. Receive timely notice of meetings and union activities
    4. Propose agenda items or call for special meetings (in accordance with bylaws)
  2. A member in good standing is any tenant who meets the eligibility criteria outlined in the Membership section, has completed a membership form, and is current on dues (if applicable).

Membership Record ¶

  1. The Secretary shall maintain an up-to-date record of members for the purposes of meeting notices, voting, and quorum verification. Personal information will be limited, confidential, and used only for union-related purposes.

Tenants' Board

The Tenants' Board of the LPM Tenants' Union shall consist of seven (7) members, elected annually by the general membership. These individuals collectively manage the affairs of the Union and represent the interests of all residents.

Duties ¶

  1. Coordinates and schedules all Annual, Special, and Tenants' Board meetings of the Union.
  2. Prepares and distributes meeting agendas in advance.
  3. Oversees nomination procedures, voting processes, and the fair administration of all Union elections.
  4. Serves as the public voice of the Union, representing tenant concerns and priorities to management, media, elected officials, and partner organizations.
  5. Supports, coordinates, and oversees the work of all standing and ad hoc committees of the Union.
  6. Oversees the collection, budgeting, and expenditure of Union funds, and ensures transparent financial records.
  7. Ensures that minutes, membership records, decisions, and documents are maintained and transferred to future boards to preserve institutional memory.

Composition of the Board ¶

Officer Roles ¶

  1. The following positions shall be filled from among the elected board members:
    1. President – Chairs meetings, represents the Union publicly, and provides organizational leadership.
    2. Vice President – Supports the President and assumes leadership duties when needed.
    3. Secretary – Maintains meeting minutes, Union records, and conducts Union elections.
    4. Communications Officer – Develops communication plan, manages social media, flyers, and updates.
    5. Treasurer (if required) – Manages any financial activities and reporting.
      1. The treasurer role may be filled as needed, depending on the Union's financial activities.
  2. Officers are chosen by a majority vote of the newly elected Tenants' Board at their first meeting following the election at the Annual Meeting.

At-Large Members

  1. All Tenants' Board members who are not elected to officer positions shall serve as At-Large Members. They contribute equally to board discussions and decision-making, and may take on special projects or lead committees.

Requirements and Expectations ¶

  1. All Board members must be members in good standing.
  2. Board members serve one-year terms, renewable by re-election.
  3. Members are expected to attend Tenants' Board meetings, participate in Union initiatives, and represent the interests of all tenants.

Committees ¶

Creation and Appointment ¶

  1. Committees may be established by:
    1. A majority vote of the Tenants' Board, or
    2. A majority vote of members present at an Annual or Special Meeting.
  2. Committee members shall be appointed by the Tenants' Board, unless otherwise determined by vote of the tenants.

Purpose and Scope ¶

  1. Committees are formed to carry out specific tasks, projects, or ongoing responsibilities that support the Union's mission and goals
  2. Each committee shall operate within the scope defined at the time of its formation and shall report its activities and recommendations to the Tenants' Board.

Reporting and Oversight ¶

  1. All committees shall:
    1. Report regularly to the Tenants' Board, including updates at Union meetings
    2. Keep brief written records of meetings and activities
    3. Submit proposals, recommendations, or expenditures to the Board for approval as needed
  2. The Tenants' Board may dissolve a committee if it is inactive or no longer needed.

Officer Support Committees ¶

  1. Union officers may form or lead a committee to assist them in fulfilling their duties. For example:
    1. The Communications Officer may lead a team to manage newsletters, flyers, or social media
    2. The Treasurer may work with a Finance Committee to prepare budgets or track expenses
  2. These officer-led committees shall remain accountable to the Tenants' Board and may include any members in good standing.

Elections ¶

Election of Tenants' Board Members

  1. Elections held at the Annual Meeting are for Tenant Board seats only (up to seven positions).
  2. Officer positions such as President, Vice-President, Secretary, Communications Officer, and Treasurer are not elected directly by general membership. Instead, they are appointed by the newly elected Board at its first meeting following the Annual Election.
  3. All Tenants' Board members shall serve a term of approximately one year, beginning at the conclusion of the Annual Meeting at which they are elected and ending at the close of the following Annual Meeting.
  4. Board members may be re-elected for additional consecutive terms without limitation.

Annual Elections ¶

  1. Elections shall be held during the Annual Meeting, open to all tenants in good standing.
  2. At least 21 days prior to the Annual Meeting, tenants may submit their names to be added to the ballot.
  3. Each nominee may submit a statement to the Secretary for circulation.
  4. Tenants may vote for as many candidates as there are open seats.
  5. Absentee Ballots must be submitted by the deadline set in meeting notice. These ballots count toward quorum and final vote results.
  6. Results are announced at the meeting and recorded in the minutes.

Eligibility to Run and Vote ¶

  1. Only current tenants in good standing may run for office or vote.

Quorum for Elections ¶

  1. To hold an election, a quorum as defined in the Meetings section must be met.
  2. If quorum is not reached, the meeting shall be adjourned and reconvened within 14 days with the same agenda; at the reconvened meeting, votes may proceed with any number of participants present.

Officer Vacancies and Removal ¶

  1. If an officer position becomes vacant between elections, the Tenants' Board may appoint a replacement by majority vote, to serve until the next Annual Meeting.
  2. An officer may be removed for cause by a two-thirds vote of members casting ballots, either at a General or Special Meeting. A minimum quorum applies.

In the Event of Fewer than 7 Candidates ¶

  1. All eligible candidates who receive votes shall be seated as Board members.
  2. The remaining seats may be filled later by appointment of the existing Board, subject to a simple majority vote.
  3. The Board may function with fewer than seven members, provided a quorum is met and duties are fulfilled.

Transparency and Best Practices ¶

  1. Elections are to be fair, transparent, and documented.
  2. Results shall be posted within one week after the Annual Meeting.
  3. The Secretary maintains all election records.

Amendments to the Bylaws ¶

  1. These bylaws may be amended by a majority vote of members in good standing at any Annual Meeting or Special Meeting.
  2. Any member in good standing may propose an amendment to the Bylaws.
  3. Proposed amendments must be submitted in writing to the Tenants' Board at least 7 days in advance of the Tenants; Board meeting. The proposal should include:
    1. The proposed language of the amendment.
    2. An explanation of the proposed amendment.
    3. Whether the vote on the proposed amendment necessitates its own Special Meeting or can wait until the next scheduled Annual Meeting or Special Meeting.
  4. The proposed amendment(s) shall be included in the meeting notice, and the full text must be made available to all members prior to the vote.
  5. Amendments take effect immediately upon approval, unless otherwise specified in the amendment text.

Dissolution of Union ¶

Procedure for Dissolution ¶

  1. The LPM Residents' Union may be dissolved by a two-thirds (⅔) vote of members in good standing, provided that:
    1. Notice of the proposed dissolution is given to all members at least 30 days in advance, and
    2. The vote is held at a general membership meeting that meets quorum requirements.
  2. The proposal must clearly state the reasons for dissolution and the intended disposition of assets.

Disposition of Funds ¶

  1. Upon dissolution, any remaining Union funds shall be used first to pay any outstanding debts or financial obligations.
  2. Any surplus funds shall be donated to a local tenants' rights organization, selected by the Tenants' Board and approved by a majority of members present at the final meeting.

Records and Materials ¶

  1. The Tenants' Board shall ensure that all official Union documents, including meeting minutes, bylaws, financial records, and contact lists, are securely archived and made available to any future organizing efforts at LPM Apartments.

Reactivation ¶

  1. Dissolution of the Union should not prevent tenants from forming a new association in the future.
  2. Reasonable access to the archived materials may be provided to assist with future tenant organizing.
