LPM Tenant Union is committed to protecting your privacy, especially given the sensitive nature of tenant organizing.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Messages sent through contact forms
  • Information you choose to share related to tenant issues

Providing information is optional, but some features may not work without it.

Automatically Collected Information

We may collect limited technical information such as:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate location (e.g., city or region)

We do not intentionally collect precise location data.

How We Use Information

We use and store personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and communications
  • Maintain membership, participation, or contact records related to tenant organizing
  • Share updates, resources, or organizing information (if you opt in)
  • Support tenant organizing, education, and internal administration
  • Improve the website and its functionality

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not provide it to third parties, except as described in this policy.

Data Sharing

We only share information when legally required to do so (e.g., court order or subpoena) or with your explicit consent. We do not share information with landlords, property managers, or real estate companies.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access. However, no online system is 100% secure. You use this site at your own risk.

Sensitive Information Warning

Do not submit highly sensitive information (such as Social Security numbers, immigration status, or detailed legal strategy) through this website.
